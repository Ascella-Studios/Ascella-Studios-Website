What Kept is — and is not

Kept is a personal tracking companion for people quitting gambling. It counts your bet-free days, estimates the money you have kept based on the spending figure you provide, and offers supportive tools like a breathing exercise and optional AI-generated insights.

Kept is not medical care, professional counseling, or a treatment program, and it makes no guarantee about any outcome. The information and estimates it shows (including the "Money Kept" total, which is calculated from your own inputs) are for personal motivation only and are not financial advice. If you are struggling, please use the free, confidential support lines listed in the app — including the National Problem Gambling Helpline (1-800-522-4700) and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — or talk to a qualified professional.
