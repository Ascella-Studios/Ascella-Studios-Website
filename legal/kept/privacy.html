Privacy Policy — Kept: Quit Gambling

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Kept: Quit Gambling ("Kept", "the app") is built by Ascella Studios. Privacy is not a feature of Kept — it is the point. This policy explains exactly what data exists, where it lives, and what leaves your device.

The short version

  • There is no account, no login, and no cloud sync. Your recovery data lives on your phone.
  • We cannot see your quit date, your spending, your check-ins, your urges, your photos, or your notes. Ever.
  • Deleting the app deletes your data.
  • If you use the optional AI features, your question and a summary of your tracking data are sent to our AI service to generate a response, and are not stored there.

Data stored on your device only

Everything you enter in Kept is stored in a local database on your device and never uploaded by us:

  • Your quit date and former weekly spending amount
  • Daily check-ins, streaks, and relapse records (including optional notes)
  • Urge log timestamps
  • Why-card photos and captions (photos are referenced from your device's storage and are never transmitted)
  • Savings goals, settings, and preferences (including your passcode-lock and appearance settings)
  • Local usage events (for example, which screens were opened) used only on-device — Kept has no analytics service and this data is not transmitted

The optional Face ID / passcode lock is handled by your device's system authentication. Kept never sees or stores your passcode or biometric data.

Optional AI features (Pro)

Kept Pro includes AI-generated insights and "the Keeper," a companion you can message. When you use these features:

  • Your question and a summary of your tracking data (recent check-ins, urge timestamps, money-kept totals) are sent over an encrypted connection to our AI processing service to generate a response.
  • This data is used only to generate the response and is not stored on our servers or used to train models.
  • Conversations with the Keeper are session-only — they are not saved, on the server or on your device.
  • You are asked for consent before first use, and you can turn AI features off entirely in Settings at any time.

The AI companion is not a person, and it is not a counselor. If you are in crisis, the app always shows free, confidential human support lines.

Third-party services

  • Apple App Store / RevenueCat — subscription purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat manages subscription status using an anonymous identifier and purchase records; it does not receive any of your recovery data. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Crash reporting (Sentry) — if a crash occurs, technical diagnostics (device model, OS version, stack trace) may be sent to help us fix bugs. Crash reports never include your recovery data.

Kept contains no advertising, no ad trackers, and no social features.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Kept permanently deletes your local database and all recovery data. There is no server copy to request deletion of, because none exists.

Children

Kept is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
